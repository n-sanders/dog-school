## Following the nose

**Scenthounds** track animals by smell. Many have **long ears** and **loose skin**
around the face, which people think may help trap scent near the nose.

## Basset Hound

- **Legs:** very short, on a long, heavy body.
- **Ears:** extremely long and velvety.
- **Face:** wrinkled brow and droopy lower eyelids, giving a sad look.

## Bloodhound

- **Size:** large and **long-legged** - much taller than a Basset.
- **Skin:** loose, falling in deep folds around the head and neck.
- **Colors:** black and tan, liver and tan, or red.

## Beagle (revisited)

You met the Beagle in Unit 2. It is a scenthound too, but has a shorter, tidier
face and normal-length legs.

## Telling them apart

- **Short legs and huge ears:** Basset Hound.
- **Tall, with lots of loose skin:** Bloodhound.
- **Small, neat, tricolor and normal legs:** Beagle.
