## Why features matter

When experts identify a dog, they do not just look at the whole dog and guess.
They check a few **features** one at a time, then put the clues together. Learning
to do this makes you much faster and more accurate.

## The five features to check first

1. **Size** - Is the dog tiny enough to carry, or as tall as a table? Size groups
   go roughly from *toy* and *small* through *medium* and *large* to *giant*.
2. **Coat** - Is the hair short and smooth, long and silky, curly, or wiry
   (rough and bristly)? What colors and patterns does it have?
3. **Ears** - *Erect* ears stand straight up. *Drop* ears hang down. Some ears are
   in between: semi-erect ears stand up with the tips folded forward.
4. **Muzzle** - The muzzle is the nose and jaw. Some breeds have a long, narrow
   muzzle; others have a short, flat face.
5. **Tail** - Is it long or short? Carried low, straight up, or curled over the back?

## Watch out

- **Puppies** often look different from adults. Their ears and coats may not have
  grown in yet.
- **Haircuts** can hide a breed's natural coat.
- **Lighting** in a photo can make colors look darker or lighter than they really are.
