## Color is a trap

Yellow Labradors and light Golden Retrievers can be exactly the same color, so
**color alone will not tell you the breed**. Instead, look at:

- **Coat length:** Lab - short and flat. Golden - medium and often wavy.
- **Feathering:** Golden - long hair on the legs, chest, belly and tail.
  Lab - none.
- **Tail:** Lab - thick "otter tail". Golden - feathered and flowing.

## Other golden dogs

- **Rough Collie and Shetland Sheepdog** can be sable (golden-brown) too, but
  they have a long, **pointed, wedge-shaped head** and a big mane. A Golden has
  a broad skull and straight muzzle.
- **Belgian Malinois** can be fawn but has a short coat, a **black mask** and
  erect ears.
