## Working partners

The German Shepherd Dog and the Belgian Malinois are both **herding** breeds
now widely used by police and the military. They have similar pointed ears and
a similar shape of head, so people mix them up.

## German Shepherd Dog

- **Build:** longer than tall, with a back that **slopes** toward the hindquarters.
- **Coat:** medium-length and thick.
- **Color:** most often black and tan or black and red, with a dark **saddle**
  across the back.
- **Tail:** bushy, hanging in a gentle curve.

## Belgian Malinois

- **Build:** square, lighter-boned and leaner, with a **level** back.
- **Coat:** **short** and straight.
- **Color:** fawn to mahogany, with black tips on the hairs, plus a black
  **mask** and black ears.

## The key clues

1. Short fawn coat and a black mask: **Malinois**.
2. Thicker coat with a black saddle and a sloping back: **German Shepherd**.
