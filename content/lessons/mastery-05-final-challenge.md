## Graduation

This is the final test. It mixes breeds from every unit, and wrong answers
include their closest look-alikes.

Before you start:

- Work through the **five features**: size, coat, ears, muzzle and tail.
- Ask what **job** the dog looks built for.
- For look-alikes, use the **key clue** you learned for that pair.

Good luck!
