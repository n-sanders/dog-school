## Size in photos

Photos make it hard to judge size. A close-up of a tiny dog can look just like a
photo of a big one. Look for **reference objects**:

- People's hands, legs or laps
- Furniture, doorways, stairs and cars
- Toys, balls and leashes
- Grass, flowers and leaves

## Whippet or Italian Greyhound?

Both have the same smooth-coated sighthound shape.

- **Whippet:** about 18 to 22 inches tall. Sturdier, with more muscle.
- **Italian Greyhound:** about 13 to 15 inches tall. Very fine, delicate bones -
  small enough to sit in a lap.

## Rough Collie or Shetland Sheepdog?

- **Rough Collie:** about 22 to 26 inches tall.
- **Shetland Sheepdog:** about 13 to 16 inches tall.

A Sheltie next to a person reaches roughly knee height; a Rough Collie reaches
much higher.
