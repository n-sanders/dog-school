## Review

| Breed | Key clue |
| --- | --- |
| Siberian Husky | Medium, light build; blue or mixed eyes |
| Alaskan Malamute | Large and heavy; always brown eyes |
| Samoyed | White fluffy coat and a "smile" |
| Bernese Mountain Dog | Black, rust and white tricolor |
| Saint Bernard | Massive head, red and white coat |
| Great Dane | Extremely tall, sleek short coat |
