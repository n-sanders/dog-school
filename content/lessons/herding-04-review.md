## Review

| Breed | Key clue |
| --- | --- |
| German Shepherd Dog | Sloping back, black saddle, bushy tail |
| Belgian Malinois | Short fawn coat, black mask, level back |
| Border Collie | Black and white, intense stare, long tail |
| Rough Collie | Large, long coat with a big mane |
| Shetland Sheepdog | Looks like a small Rough Collie |
| Pembroke Welsh Corgi | Short legs, short or no tail |
| Cardigan Welsh Corgi | Short legs, long brush tail |
| Australian Shepherd | Merle coat, often blue or odd eyes, short tail |
