## Unit 8: Look-alikes

You have now studied every unit. In Unit 8 you will practice the pairs of
breeds that people confuse most often.

## Husky or Malamute?

| Clue | Siberian Husky | Alaskan Malamute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Size and build** | Medium, light, built for speed | Large, heavy-boned, built for pulling |
| **Eyes** | Blue, brown, or one of each | Always brown |
| **Ears** | Set close together, high on the head | Small for the head, set wide apart |
| **Tail** | Sickle curve, not tightly curled | Plume waving over the back |

## Strategy

1. **Check the eyes first.** Blue eyes rule out a Malamute.
2. **Then check the size and bone.** A big, bulky dog with a broad head is more
   likely a Malamute.
3. **Finally, the ears.** Wide-set small ears point to Malamute; close-set ears
   point to Husky.
