## Westie or Maltese?

| Clue | West Highland White Terrier | Maltese |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Coat texture** | Harsh and straight | Long, soft and silky |
| **Ears** | Small, pointed, **erect** | **Drop** ears hidden in long hair |
| **Tail** | Short "carrot" tail straight up | Carried over the back |
| **Build** | Sturdy terrier | Very small and delicate |

## Westie or Cairn?

The Cairn Terrier is never white, but light wheaten Cairns can look pale in
photos. A Westie is **pure white** with a neatly rounded face; a Cairn is
**shaggier**, often with darker ears and muzzle.

## Other white dogs

A **Samoyed** is white too, but it is a much larger spitz with a very fluffy,
stand-off coat and a curled tail.
