## Review

You have learned six popular family breeds. Before the quiz, remind yourself of
each breed's key clue:

| Breed | Key clue |
| --- | --- |
| Labrador Retriever | Short, flat, solid-colored coat; thick otter tail |
| Golden Retriever | Golden coat with feathering on legs and tail |
| Beagle | Small tricolor hound with drop ears and a white tail tip |
| Standard Poodle | Curly coat, long muzzle, proud posture |
| Bulldog | Heavy wrinkled head, low wide body, small folded ears |
| French Bulldog | Small bulldog with big upright bat ears |

After this review, you can choose any of the next five units in any order.
