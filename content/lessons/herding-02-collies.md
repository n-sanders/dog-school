## Collie-type dogs

Collies come from Scotland and northern England, where they herded sheep. They
are clever, quick and often have long, flowing coats.

## Border Collie

- **Color:** usually **black and white**, with white on the face, neck, legs and
  tail tip.
- **Coat:** smooth (short) or rough (medium and feathered).
- **Build:** athletic and slightly longer than tall, with a long, low tail.
- **Look:** an intense, focused stare - the famous "herding eye".

## Rough Collie

- **Size:** large - about 22 to 26 inches at the shoulder.
- **Coat:** very long, with a big **mane** and frill around the neck.
- **Head:** long and wedge-shaped, with semi-erect ears that tip forward.

## Shetland Sheepdog (Sheltie)

- **Size:** small - about 13 to 16 inches at the shoulder.
- **Coat and head:** almost the same as the Rough Collie's - long coat, full mane,
  wedge-shaped head with tipped ears.

## The key clue

The Rough Collie and the Sheltie look like big and small versions of each other.
**Size is the main clue.** In a photo, look for something to compare against,
like a person, a chair or a doorway.
