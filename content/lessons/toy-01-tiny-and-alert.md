## Chihuahua

- **Size:** the smallest breed - no more than 6 pounds.
- **Head:** a rounded **"apple" head** with a short, slightly pointed muzzle.
- **Ears and eyes:** very large, erect ears and big, round eyes.
- **Coat:** smooth or long, in almost any color.

## Papillon

- **Ears:** large, **fringed** with long hair, spreading like **butterfly wings**.
- **Color:** white with colored patches; the face is often colored with a white
  stripe (blaze) down the middle.
- **Coat:** long and silky, with a plumed tail arched over the back.

## Pomeranian

- **Coat:** thick and **puffy**, standing out from the body, with a big ruff
  around the neck.
- **Face:** fox-like, with a short muzzle and small erect ears.
- **Tail:** heavily plumed and carried flat over the back.
- **Color:** most often orange or red.

## Telling them apart

- **Long-haired Chihuahua or Papillon?** Look at the ears. Papillon ears have
  long fringes like wings, and the coat is usually white with patches.
- **Papillon or Pomeranian?** A Pomeranian looks like a round puffball with
  *small* ears; a Papillon has *big* fringed ears and a flatter, silkier coat.
