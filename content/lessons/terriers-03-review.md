## Review

| Breed | Key clue |
| --- | --- |
| West Highland White Terrier | All white, round face, carrot tail |
| Scottish Terrier | Beard and eyebrows, very low body, often black |
| Cairn Terrier | Shaggy, foxy face, never white |
| Airedale Terrier | Largest terrier, tan with black saddle, wiry coat |
| Russell Terrier | Small, mostly white with tan or black patches |
| Yorkshire Terrier | Tiny, long silky blue-and-tan coat |
